Property Overview

Set above 50+ mile Blue Ridge Mountain views, Belle View is a 10,600± sq ft mountain residence on 15 private acres situated among Virginia’s renowned wine country. Currently enjoyed as a private residence, the property also carries a remarkable history and even greater operational potential. Originally built in 1996 and expanded over time, the estate is currently enjoyed primarily as a private residence while operating at limited short-term rental capacity. The lodge includes 8 private guest accommodations with en-suite bathrooms and a 3 bedroom apartment unit, alongside a separate 3-bedroom owner’s residence, offering flexibility for owner-operators, on-site management, or hybrid residential and hospitality use.

Consistent with its historical use as both an assisted living facility and a recovery lodge operated by Centra Health, the estate was designed to support live-in occupancy with the institutional-grade systems, robust utilities, and a layout that naturally accommodates group stays, retreats, and guest turnover. Beyond lodging, the property has also attracted weddings, retreats, and private events with little formal advertising due to its one-of-a-kind backdrop and existing event infrastructure. The property is undoubtedly equipped to support weddings and large-scale gatherings, featuring multiple ceremony location options, a dedicated bridal suite, ample on-site parking, a secondary building with bar and restroom facilities, and a 40×60 concrete slab featuring integrated tent anchoring and pre-fabricated engineering for future structural expansion if desired.

Whether envisioned as a fully scaled short-term rental / boutique hospitality concept, an operational winery destination, retreat property, or wedding venue, the groundwork is already in place for a buyer ready to capitalize on its exceptional potential—or simply continue enjoying it as a serene mountain home.
